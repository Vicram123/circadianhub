Which nap length to pick

A 20-minute nap is the safe default for an afternoon dip. A 90-minute nap suits days when you have time for a full cycle. Longer naps taken late in the day can make it harder to fall asleep that night.

Timing matters

Early to mid afternoon is usually the best window. Set an alarm for the wake time shown, and avoid napping within a few hours of your usual bedtime.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a nap be?

For most people, about 20 minutes for a quick boost or about 90 minutes for a full cycle. Anything in between risks waking from deep sleep.

Does a coffee nap work?

Some people drink coffee just before a short nap so the caffeine starts working as they wake. Individual results vary, and it is best avoided late in the day.

Will napping ruin my night sleep?

Short, early naps rarely do. Long or late naps can push back your bedtime, especially if you already struggle to fall asleep.
