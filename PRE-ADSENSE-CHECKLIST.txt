IMPORTANT: Google-certified consent management (EEA/Finland)
------------------------------------------------------------
A static website package cannot activate a Google-certified CMP on your AdSense account. Before serving ads to EEA visitors, sign in to AdSense and go to Privacy & messaging, create/configure the relevant European regulations message using Google's certified CMP, and install the exact generated code on the site. Do not leave the custom placeholder consent banner as the production consent solution, and do not load advertising scripts before consent where consent is required. Test accept, reject, and reopening settings on a deployed site.
