How the estimate works

Estimated shortfall = max(0, target sleep − actual sleep) × number of days. For example, averaging 6.5 hours against an 8-hour target for 7 days gives an estimated shortfall of 10.5 hours.

This is not a literal account that can be repaid hour-for-hour. Recovery varies; protect sufficient sleep opportunity rather than trying to catch up in one long sleep.

When to seek help

If you regularly cannot get enough sleep, feel dangerously sleepy during the day, or have persistent insomnia, speak with a healthcare professional. Do not drive or operate machinery when sleepy.
